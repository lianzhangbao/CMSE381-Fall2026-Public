# Credits and sources

**Textbook.** Most datasets, examples and figures in these materials come from James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani, Taylor, *An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Applications in Python* (ISLP, Springer), free to download at [statlearning.com](https://www.statlearning.com/). Each day's page lists the textbook chapter it follows, and the last slide of each deck repeats the reference.

**Figures.** Figures taken from the textbook are credited to it. Where a slide shows a screenshot of the book, please read the chapter itself for the original and its full context.

**Spam example (Day 1).** Table 1.1 from Hastie, Tibshirani and Friedman, *The Elements of Statistical Learning*, 2nd ed., Springer.

**Data.** Datasets are loaded from the ISLP Python package or from the textbook website. They are not re-hosted in this repository.

**Course materials.** Slides and notebooks are by Prof. Lianzhang Bao, adapted from earlier CMSE 381 course materials.
